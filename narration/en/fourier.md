# Chapter 5 fourier

## fourier

A more rigorous route proves it through Fourier series and Parseval's identity. A function expands into a superposition of sines and cosines, and as long as it is square integrable, the integral of the square of the function equals the sum of the squares of its coefficients. Here we only have to expand f of x equal to x to get the result we need.
